Why not the 3D glassy icons. Three collisions with brand/DESIGN.md, none of them about the colour: gradients are banned outright and 3D glass is a gradient by construction; the whole positioning is that client work is the only colour, so ours is none; and the v3 rebrand moved us away from violet-on-dark specifically because it read as a SaaS tool. Separately, the ambient budget is spent — the hero aura is the one ambient layer, and a second was deleted on 4 Sept because two behind one headline fight each other. And the reference set is stock, on a site selling bespoke creative.

Where this ended up

  • E is the one. One graphic for the section, no ornament in the cells. It is built from the monogram’s own measured geometry, so it is unmistakably from the same hand as the mark and cannot be lifted off a stock site. DESIGN.md already asks for one signature moment per surface, not five.
  • B failed and it was mine. A camera, signal arcs, a card stack and a refresh arrow — standard glyph vocabulary drawn by hand. Worse, all of it round: circles, arcs and rounded caps, when the mark has no curves at all. Off-language by construction, not just generic.
  • D failed too, and the failure is the useful part. The monogram’s edges were measured rather than eyeballed: 40.2% at 50° from vertical, 21.0% at 45°, 24.3% horizontal, 10.8% at 85°, and effectively no verticals. That vocabulary makes marks that are wide and flat. Tall icons and tall numerals both need verticals, so anything forced into a 74px square comes out as horizontal slashes — which is what D rendered.
  • So the geometry chose the format. Once the constraint was measured instead of assumed, a wide horizontal composition was the only honest thing to build with it. Four planes going out, two folding back, wiping left to right.
  • A and C are kept for the comparison, not as candidates. C solved the wrong problem — it optimised for cheapness to maintain, which for a creative agency is the wrong variable. Drawing our own assets is the demonstration, not a cost to avoid.
  • Not about taste, the 3D route: gradients are banned in DESIGN.md and 3D glass is a gradient by construction; client work is the only colour, so ours is none; and v3 moved off violet-on-dark because it read as a SaaS tool.
