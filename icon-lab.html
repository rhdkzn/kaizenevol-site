Why not the 3D glassy icons. Three collisions with brand/DESIGN.md, none of them about the colour: gradients are banned outright and 3D glass is a gradient by construction; the whole positioning is that client work is the only colour, so ours is none; and the v3 rebrand moved us away from violet-on-dark specifically because it read as a SaaS tool. Separately, the ambient budget is spent — the hero aura is the one ambient layer, and a second was deleted on 4 Sept because two behind one headline fight each other. And the reference set is stock, on a site selling bespoke creative.

What looking at it actually shows

  • C wins, and it is the cheapest of the three. Diego’s complaint is that the section has no life. C gives it life — the numeral writes itself in and then inks solid — using the mark that is already on the site. Nothing new to draw, nothing new to maintain, nothing else on the page has to change.
  • B does not win, and I built B. Three of the four marks are standard glyph vocabulary: a camera, signal arcs, a card stack, a refresh arrow. Drawing them by hand does not make them bespoke — it makes them a hand-traced icon set. That is the same criticism I levelled at the stock reference, so it would be dishonest to ship it as the answer.
  • What B did prove is that the motion is the asset. A mark that draws itself cannot be lifted off a stock site, because it is a behaviour rather than a file. That is the part worth keeping, and C keeps it without the asset debt.
  • Why not the 3D glassy icons is a separate question, and it is already settled above — three collisions with DESIGN.md, none about taste.
  • The real cost of B, if we ever want it: four marks drawn by us per section, forever, every time a section is added or reworded. Stock is free and instant. That is the actual trade.
