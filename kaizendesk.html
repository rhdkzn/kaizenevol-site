Questions, Answered

Will my customers know they're talking to an AI?
Yes. KaizenDesk introduces itself as your business's assistant — it never pretends to be a person. It sounds natural, answers their questions, books the job, and hands the real ones straight to you. Customers get a fast, helpful reply instead of a missed call.
Do I need to change my phone number or learn any software?
No. KaizenDesk connects to your existing number and email. You don't install anything or change how you work. Your phone only buzzes when there's a genuine job to take.
How quickly can it be up and running?
Days, not weeks. A 15-minute chat about your business, services and opening hours is all we need. We switch it on, and you carry on working.
What happens if a customer asks something it can't handle?
It answers what it can, qualifies the enquiry, and flags anything that needs you straight to your phone. You stay in control of every real job — KaizenDesk just makes sure none of them slip through.
Is my customer data safe?
Yes. KaizenDesk is UK-based and fully GDPR-compliant, with your data held in the EU. You keep your number, your reviews, and your customer relationships — nothing is locked away from you.
Which businesses is KaizenDesk for?
Any local business that takes enquiries by phone, text or email — plumbers, electricians, builders, dentists, salons, gyms, garages, restaurants and more. If you lose work to missed calls and slow replies, it's built for you.
