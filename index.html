The problem

Your craft isn’t the problem. It just isn’t in front of enough people.

The people who find it buy it. And that’s the frustrating part. The work does its job the moment someone sees it. There just aren’t enough someones. So the people who would buy it never do.

You’re a maker who has to be a marketer, and it’s the marketing that slips.

So you craft and create, put it out, and find out how it did at the same time as everyone else. When it goes well you can’t say why, and you can’t do it again. A good week you can’t repeat isn’t a plan.

The work was never the question.

The aim

Getting seen is half of it. Staying seen is the half nobody talks about.

Anyone can have a good week.

But you already know what that’s worth, because you’ve had them.

What we’re after is something else. New people finding your work every day, buying, and coming back without us paying an ad to reach them twice. That’s growth you keep. Everything else you have to buy again next month.

And we track it, because a month with more sales in it only counts if the next one does it again. If you’re the one making it, the making stays yours and the selling stops being the part you dread.

Your fix

The Kaizen Loop

First we learn the brand properly. What you make, why you make it, and what it’s meant to mean to the person who buys it. Then, before a pound is spent, two numbers: what a customer is actually worth to you, and what your sales were already doing without us.

Everything after that gets judged against both.

If you’re an artist there’s often no unit to cost, so the numbers are different. We measure what your audience does when you ask them for something, and how much of your streaming sits on one track. That number coming down is the difference between a fanbase and a song that did well.

What we run

One monthly fee. Here’s everything in it.

01

Content

We shoot it with you, or build from what you’ve already got. Concepts, hooks and edits coming consistently, not one polished piece a month, shot to the standard your work deserves, because for most people who see it the ad is the brand.

02

Ads

Run wherever your buyers actually are, not just where it’s easy. Budgets, targeting and testing handled every day, turning off what loses and putting more behind what wins, so more of the right people land on the product.

03

Social

Your feed and stories, run for you. It brings people in on its own, and it’s what convinces the ones who found you through an ad that you’re worth buying from.

04

Bringing people back

The person who already bought is the easiest sale you’ll ever make, and most brands never go back to them. We keep in touch for you, by email and message, so they come back on their own instead of you paying an ad to find them twice.

The business is still yours

We don’t choose how it looks.

The look and the direction stay yours. We’re in it with you, whether it’s a shoot or an edit, but what the brand is and where it’s going is your call and stays that way.

We make more of what you already make, put it in front of people who’ve never seen you, and if a piece doesn’t look like you it’s wrong, and we’ll say so before you have to.

And it’s not just the look. Your ad account, your content, your customer list and the tracking we set up on your shop are all yours. Your ad money goes straight from your own account, never through us. If we part ways, all of it stays with you.

How we charge

No cut of your ad spend.

One monthly payment, separate from your ad spend, so nothing we recommend is ever because it pays us more. There’s no minimum ad spend. What you pay depends on what you sell and how much content you need, so we work it out with you, but you’ll hear it on the first call, not the third.

Who this is for

This isn’t for everyone.

  • You won’t look at your own numbers. Then nobody can fix them, us included.
  • The margin’s too thin for ads to pay for themselves. We’d be spending your money to prove arithmetic.
  • You want the ads run and nothing else. That’s one of four things we do, so you’d be paying for three you don’t want.
The first five

We’re taking five clients to start.

Just the two of us. We make the work and run it ourselves, so there’s no account manager between you and the people doing it.

We don’t have client results to show yet, and we won’t borrow someone else’s. That’s why the first five pay a founding price. If you’d rather wait for proof, wait. The founding price won’t.

Tell us what you sell and what it costs you to make. Fifteen minutes tells us whether we can help, and if we can’t we’ll say so. Either way you’ll leave knowing what a customer is actually worth to you, which is more than most people get from a call.
