The problem

You’ve had the month that looked incredible and felt like nothing.

The drop sold out. The print run went. The return on ad spend was the best you’d ever seen. Then you looked at the bank and couldn’t work out where it all went. That isn’t you being bad with money. The platform counts your sales and nobody counts your costs, so every decision you make about ads, you make half blind. You can run like that for years. Most people do. And if you make the thing yourself, selling it starts to feel like a second job you’re somehow bad at.

The method

The Kaizen Loop

This is where the guessing stops. Two steps before a single pound is spent: we work out what one sale really makes you, and what your sales were already doing without ads. Then every piece of content and every pound of spend is judged against those two numbers, every week. You will know, on a Monday, whether last week’s ads made you money. Most brand owners have never once known that. Artists almost never.

01

Find your real profit per sale

What one sale leaves you after the item, the packaging, the postage and the fees are paid. Across everything you sell, not just the bestseller.

02

Mark where you already are

What your sales were already doing before us, allowing for the time of year. We don't take credit for a trend you were already on.

03

Make the ads

Ideas, cuts and new versions from your own footage, your product and your work. Each one is made to make you money, not to win a like.

04

Run the ads to that number

Every pound of ad spend is judged on what the sale actually left you, not on the return the platform reports. You see the same numbers we see, read the same way.

How we charge

One retainer. No cut of your ad spend.

An agency paid a percentage of your ad spend is paid to make you spend more. That’s why the last one wanted a bigger budget every month and never once asked what your product costs you. We don’t take a cut. We can’t win unless you do.

Who this is for

Who we can make money for, and who we can’t.

Built for you if
  • You’ve got something people already buy, a product, a print, a piece, a record, and orders coming in. Not an idea. Orders.
  • You can tell us what one of them costs to make, pack and post. Or you’ll find out this week.
  • You’re done paying one company to make the ads and another to blame for the results.
  • You’d rather hear “stop” from us than “best month ever” from a dashboard that isn’t counting your costs.
Not if
  • You haven’t picked what you’re selling. There’s no margin to find in a product that doesn’t exist yet, and we won’t pretend there is.
  • You won’t look at your own numbers. Then nobody can fix them, us included.
  • You just want someone to press the buttons on Meta. Cheaper people exist for that, and the price is the whole reason.
  • You need it turned round by the end of the month. The first two steps take weeks, and skipping them is how you got here.

If the first list made you feel something, that’s the point. Every month you keep spending because the platform’s number went up, you pay to learn this lesson again, with money you already earned. Ten minutes decides whether you keep doing that.
