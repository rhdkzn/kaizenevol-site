The problem

Most brands scale a number that isn't profit.

Platform ROAS counts the sale and ignores what it cost you to make, pack and ship. Scale on it and revenue can grow every month while the bank balance goes the other way - a drop that sells out at no margin is a busy way to lose money. The first thing we do is work out what a customer is genuinely worth, because every decision after that depends on it.

The method

The Kaizen Acquisition System

Two steps set the floor, once. Two steps run above it, every week. Nobody touches the ad account until step three, and the order is the point.

01

Establish the margin

Product-level costs, weighted across the catalogue, landed. What a sale actually leaves you with once everything is paid for.

02

Set the baseline

What your revenue was already doing before us, adjusted for season. We do not take credit for a trend you were already on.

03

Build the creative

Concepts, hooks and iteration built from your own footage and product photography, briefed off what the account is telling us.

04

Buy against it

Paid acquisition managed to contribution margin, with the numbers open to you and read the same way we read them.

What we run

One retainer, four systems.

01

Creative production

Statics, motion and edited brand footage. Enough volume to actually learn what works, not one hero asset a month.

02

Paid acquisition

Meta and TikTok, judged on blended performance and contribution rather than per-channel vanity numbers.

03

Organic social

04

Retention

Email and SMS flows so the customers you already paid for come back without paying for them twice.
