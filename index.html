The problem

Most people scale a number that isn't profit.

Platform ROAS counts the sale and ignores what it cost you to make, pack and ship. Scale on it and revenue can grow every month while the bank balance goes the other way - a drop that sells out at no margin is a busy way to lose money. The first thing we do is work out what a customer is genuinely worth, because every decision after that depends on it.

The method

The Kaizen Acquisition System

Two steps set the floor, once. Two steps run above it, every week. Nobody touches the ad account until step three, and the order is the point.

01

Establish the margin

Unit costs, weighted across everything you sell, landed. What a sale actually leaves you with once everything is paid for.

02

Set the baseline

What your revenue was already doing before us, adjusted for season. We do not take credit for a trend you were already on.

03

Build the creative

Concepts, hooks and iteration built from your own footage, product shots and work, briefed off what the account is telling us.

04

Buy against it

Paid acquisition managed to contribution margin, with the numbers open to you and read the same way we read them.

What we run

01

How we charge

One retainer. No cut of your ad spend.

An agency paid a percentage of spend is being paid to spend more. That is the wrong thing to hand someone whose job is protecting your margin, so we do not take one.

01

One fee

Creative, paid acquisition, social and retention on a single monthly retainer. No per-channel invoices, no add-on for the thing you assumed was included.

02

Your budget stays yours

You pay Meta and TikTok directly from your own account. We never hold your media money, and you watch every pound of it go out.

03

You own the work

Ad account, pixel, creative, customer data, list. If we part ways it all stays with you, and none of it needs asking for.

04

Who this is for

Built for you if — and honestly, not if.

Built for you if
  • You make and sell your own work, and there is something a stranger could buy today.
  • You will show us the real costs — what one unit takes to make, pack and ship.
  • You want the creative and the buying done by the same people, because the next batch is only better if whoever briefs it has read the account.
  • You would rather be told to stop spending than be told what you want to hear.
Not if
  • You want someone to run ads against creative you supply. That is a different job, and other people do it well.
  • You are still deciding what to sell. There is no margin to find in a product that does not exist yet.
  • You would rather not look at your own numbers. The whole method is numbers.
  • You need it working this month. The margin and the baseline come first, and that is weeks before we spend properly.
