What you are not risking

Four things we will not do to you.

  • We never take a cut of your ad spend. It leaves your account, not ours, so nothing we suggest is because it pays us more. No minimum either.
  • We say no on the first call, not three months in. If the numbers do not work, you hear it while it is still free.
  • Nothing is held hostage. The ad account, the content and the customer list are yours the whole time. Stop whenever you like and they stay with you.
  • You get an answer either way. We read every enquiry ourselves and reply the same working day, including the ones we turn down.

This isn’t for everyone.

  • You won’t look at your own numbers. Then nobody can fix them, us included.
  • The margin’s too thin for ads to pay for themselves. We’d be spending your money to prove arithmetic.
  • You want the ads run and nothing else. That’s one of four things we do, so you’d be paying for three you don’t want.

You do not have to be good at any of this.

Most of the people we talk to think they are behind. They are not posting enough, the last ads did nothing, the shop has been half-finished for a year. None of that counts against you here, because it is the job.

If you could have fixed it yourself in the evenings, you already would have.
