Common Questions

Straight answers. No fluff.

Everything You Need to Know

If something’s not covered here, book a call and ask directly. We’ll give you a straight answer.

What exactly do you do for me?

Everything. We build your campaigns from scratch — copy, creatives, targeting, and the qualification funnel that filters leads before they reach you. Once it’s live, we optimise daily and send you a simple report every week. You show up to the consultations we put in your calendar. That’s your job. Everything else is ours.

What results can I realistically expect?

Multiple qualified, booked renovation clients per month — each with a high project value. We don’t count enquiries or unqualified leads as results. We count booked consultations with homeowners who have a real budget and a real project. Our guarantee: your agreed monthly target (typically 2–4 booked consultations per month depending on your trade, area, and ad spend), delivered within 60 days — or your management fee is waived.

How long until I see results?

Campaigns go live within 7 days of the discovery call. The system is designed to deliver booked jobs within 60 days. We’re testing and cutting underperforming ads, scaling what’s working, and tightening the funnel throughout. You’re not waiting months to see movement.

How much of my time does this take?

Minimal. You’ll spend 30 minutes on the discovery call, then a short review session when we present the campaign before launch — that’s it for setup. After that, the only time you spend is on the consultations we book into your calendar. We handle reporting, optimisation, and all communication with leads.

How much do I need to spend on ads?

It depends on your local market, how competitive your area is, and your monthly client targets. We agree on the right figure together during the discovery call — nothing is set without your sign-off. Your ad spend is paid directly to Meta by you. We don’t hold the budget, take a cut, or mark it up.

What counts as a “qualified lead”?

A qualified lead is a homeowner who has passed through our intake funnel — they’ve confirmed they own the property, have a genuine renovation project in mind, have a realistic budget, and are looking to move forward within a reasonable timeframe. Renters, tyre-kickers, and people with no budget don’t make it through. You only see people who are serious.

What if I’ve tried Meta ads before and it didn’t work?

Failed Meta ads almost always fail for the same reasons: generic copy, wrong audience, no qualification funnel, and no one actually watching the data. Most agencies run the same campaigns for every client. This system is purpose-built for your trade — whether you remodel kitchens, renovate bathrooms, or build loft conversions, the targeting, copy, and funnel are all specific to your niche. Book a call and we’ll walk through exactly what went wrong last time.

Why do you only work with one specialist per niche per area?

Because we’d be competing against ourselves if we didn’t. Running the same ad system for two kitchen remodellers in the same town would drive up costs, dilute results, and put us in the uncomfortable position of deciding whose campaign to favour. One client per niche per area means our interests are fully aligned with yours. A kitchen specialist and a bathroom specialist in the same postcode aren’t competitors, so each can hold their own territory — but once you sign, no other business in your trade and local market gets access to this.

Do you do anything besides ads?

Yes. KaizenReach is our ad service for renovation specialists. KaizenDesk is our AI front office for any local business — it answers every call, text and email 24/7, books the job, and chases your Google reviews, so the enquiries you win never slip through. The two work together, or each on its own.
