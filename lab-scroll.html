What is being tested here, and what is still missing.

  • The scroll is not hijacked. The wheel keeps doing what the wheel does — a tall spacer drives the track sideways, so the page never fights you.
  • On a phone the mechanism switches off entirely and it becomes a native swipe carousel. Overriding scroll on touch is how a page starts feeling broken.
  • The icons are ours, not stock. What came through was a screenshot of a Google Images grid, so there were no files in it at any usable size. These are generated in the same technique as those references — glossy 3D, soft studio light, bevelled forms — but in the site’s own palette rather than the violet the v3 rebrand moved away from. Say the word and they get regenerated in colour.
  • Their scale is measured, not eyeballed. Each icon’s ink bounding box was read off a canvas — spans of 713, 773, 772 and 737 at 1024px — and normalised so all four carry the same optical weight in the slot.
  • 2.1MB of PNG became 20KB of WebP at the size actually displayed, with a PNG fallback. A homepage does not get to be heavy.
  • Cards dim as they leave. The one in the middle is the one you are reading, so there is always a focus rather than four things competing.
