[ 01 / The leak ]

Leads were never
the problem.

Every agency in this trade sells the same thing. They send you enquiries, they report on cost per lead, and what happens next is your problem.

So the enquiries land while you are under a sink. Three of them go to voicemail. Two get a text back at nine that evening, by which point they have already booked the firm that answered. The lead was never lost at the top. It was lost at the door.

That is why we do not sell traffic on its own. A machine that fills the top and leaks at the bottom is a more expensive version of the problem you already had.

[ 02 / The system ]

Three stages. Built to compound.

Run in order. Each one is worth more because the one before it is already standing.

01
Stage one

Foundation

Free with your retainer

We build the site your ads point at, and we hold the backend. That is what lets us prove which job came from which advert, instead of asking you to take our word for it.

  • A site built to sell the job, not to win a design award
  • Live within 24 hours of you approving it
  • We host it, we maintain it, we fix it. No waiting on a developer
  • Every enquiry tracked from first click through to booked job
  • You own your domain. Always, and in writing
02
Stage two

Demand

This is KaizenReach

Paid Meta advertising and your social accounts, run for you, on one flat monthly fee. This is the engine, and it is the part you are actually buying.

  • Meta campaigns built for your trade and your postcodes
  • Creative cut from footage of your real jobs, tested weekly
  • Instagram and Facebook run for you, comments and messages included
  • Google Business Profile kept current, and reviews chased
  • One client per trade, per area. Cancel any time
03
Stage three

Capture

This is KaizenDesk, a separate service

The front office that answers while you are on the tools. Stage two makes the phone ring; this is the stage that decides whether ringing turns into money.

  • Answers every enquiry in seconds, at any hour
  • Books the job straight into your diary
  • Goes back to the ones that went quiet
  • Flags the hot ones to you the moment they land
  • Priced separately, and only worth having once stage two is running
[ 03 / Why it holds ]

We can show you
which advert paid.

The oldest argument in this industry is whether the marketing worked. Most agencies answer it backwards, reconstructing the story afterwards from whatever numbers they can reach.

We own the site the enquiries land on. So we are not reconstructing anything. We can follow a booked kitchen back to the advert, the day and the postcode that produced it.

That is the real reason stage one exists, and it is why we give the site away rather than sell it. If we ever stop working together, you can buy it outright and keep it running.
