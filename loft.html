Questions Worth Asking

The ones we'd expect before anyone books a call with us.

What if I've tried Meta ads before and they didn't work?

Failed Meta ads almost always fail for the same reasons: generic copy, wrong audience, no qualification funnel, and no one actually watching the data. Most agencies run the same campaigns for every client. This system is purpose-built for your trade — whether you remodel kitchens, renovate bathrooms, or build loft conversions, the targeting, copy, and funnel are all specific to your niche. Book a call and we'll walk through exactly what went wrong last time.

How much do I need to spend on ads?

It depends on your local market, how competitive your area is, and your monthly client targets. We agree on the right figure together during the discovery call — nothing is set without your sign-off. Your ad spend is paid directly to Meta by you. We don't hold the budget, take a cut, or mark it up.

What results can I realistically expect?

Multiple qualified, booked renovation clients per month, each with a high project value. We don't count enquiries or unqualified leads as results. We count booked consultations with homeowners who have a real budget and a real project. Our guarantee: your agreed monthly target, delivered within 60 days — or your management fee is waived.

How much of my time does this take?

Minimal. You'll spend 30 minutes on the discovery call, then a short review session when we present the campaign before launch. After that, the only time you spend is on the consultations we book into your calendar. We handle reporting, optimisation, and all communication with leads.

Why do you only work with one specialist per niche per area?

Because we'd be competing against ourselves if we didn't. Running the same system for two kitchen remodellers in the same town would drive up costs, dilute results, and put us in the uncomfortable position of deciding whose campaign to favour. One client per niche per area means our interests are fully aligned with yours. A kitchen specialist and a bathroom specialist in the same postcode aren't competitors, so each can hold their own territory — but once you sign, no other business in your trade and local market gets access to this.
