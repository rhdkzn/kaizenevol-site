Where the furniture comes from

To provide you with the best products we have developed relationships with top kitchen suppliers and continually refer to them for training and information regarding the high quality products we offer. Our “cutting edge” knowledge is then passed on directly as a key benefit for our customers.

Mereway Kitchens — Premium Partner

We are a Mereway Premium Partner. Their furniture is constructed using traditional materials and methods and is hand crafted in the UK — which is why it’s on our showroom floor.

Karndean Designflooring — Retailer

We are a Karndean retailer, so the flooring can be specified, supplied and fitted as part of the same job — not left to you to arrange afterwards.

Also designed, supplied and installed

We can also design, supply and install en-suites, cloakrooms, utility rooms, lifestyle, home and office furniture. A supply only service for furniture, appliances, flooring and so on is also available.

Full project management is available for you, including building works, structural alterations and other interior design elements such as lighting and flooring.
