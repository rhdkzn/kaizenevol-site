What we do

Everything your car needs, under one roof.

All makes and models serviced and repaired, by trained staff who tell you the cost before any work starts.

MOT Testing

MOT testing five days a week. If your vehicle needs work to pass, you get a clear explanation and a quote first. We do not charge for the MOT retest after the work has been completed by us.

Servicing

Professional maintenance work with no loss of manufacturer warranty, for both used and new cars. Service formats to suit both your vehicle and your pocket, including manufacturer service schedules for newer vehicles.

Diagnostics

We have all the equipment and technical staff needed to resolve your diagnostic queries. Electronics are responsible for a high percentage of engine management issues — so we keep both the staff and the kit to deal with them.

Repairs

Suspension, steering, water pump, electrics, heaters, windows, engine rebuilds, gearbox, clutch and dual mass flywheel, exhaust systems, brakes, ABS, shock absorbers and brake fluid changes.

Air Conditioning

Air conditioning checks, services and re-gas — so the system works when the weather turns and the screen clears when it needs to.

Diesel Particulate Filter

DPF repair and regeneration, plus locking wheel nut removal and replacement, and fuel contamination resolution.

Courtesy cars

Book your MOT and Service together and take advantage of our courtesy cars.

Allow 28 days prior to your expiry date

The Ministry of Transport allows you to test your vehicle up to 28 days prior to your expiry date without any time being lost. It is recommended that you have your MOT done early, in case any faults are found.

MOT reminders for existing customers

If you have your service and MOT carried out at our garage, we will send out a yearly reminder by email or text message before your MOT and service is due.
