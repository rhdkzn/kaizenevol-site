Not every car needs the same treatment — and not every driver has the same goal. Whether you're after a reliable clean that keeps your car presentable, a proper deep-clean restoration, or a full transformation with lasting protection, choosing the right package from the start means you get exactly what your car needs without overpaying for work it doesn't require.

Start Here: What's the Current Condition of Your Car?

The right package depends almost entirely on your car's starting point. A car that's been regularly looked after needs a different approach to one that's come through a Bristol winter on salted roads with nothing done to it since autumn.

A quick self-assessment before you book:

  • Recently valeted and just needs a refresh: Premium Valeting is likely all you need.
  • A few months since the last proper clean, some surface contamination present: Gold Detailing will reset things properly.
  • Hasn't had serious attention in a while, or you want maximum protection from the outset: Diamond Detailing is the right choice.
  • Something specific — fleet vehicles, show prep, or a bespoke requirement: Custom Project, discussed directly with us.

Premium Valeting — £97

The Premium Valet is a thorough, professional clean of your vehicle inside and out — performed with the care and products that distinguish proper valeting from a petrol station jet wash.

What's included:

  • Pre-rinse and snow foam pre-wash
  • Safe two-bucket contact wash with pH-neutral shampoo
  • Wheel and tyre cleaning, tyre dressing applied
  • Full interior vacuum, dashboard and surfaces wiped, glass cleaned inside and out
  • Exterior plastic and trim treatment
  • Quick detailer applied for shine and light protection

The Premium Valet is the practical starting point — particularly if your car is in reasonable condition and you want to establish a regular maintenance routine. Booked every 6–8 weeks, this package keeps your car in consistently good shape and works out cheaper over a year than a weekly drive-through car wash. See our comparison of mobile valeting versus automatic car washes for the full breakdown.

Gold Detailing — £197

The Gold Detail goes significantly further. Where the Premium Valet cleans, the Gold Detail decontaminates and restores.

Everything in the Premium Valet, plus:

  • Iron fallout decontamination treatment — removes brake dust and industrial particles bonded to the paint that a wash alone cannot shift
  • Clay bar decontamination — restores a genuinely smooth surface by lifting embedded contamination
  • Full interior deep clean including seat and carpet extraction where needed
  • Application of a quality paint sealant for extended protection (typically 3–4 months)

The Gold Detail is the most popular choice for drivers who want their car to feel genuinely reset — particularly those returning from a Bristol winter of road salt, or anyone whose car hasn't had professional attention for several months. Our guide on how Bristol's climate damages paintwork explains why decontamination matters more than most people realise.

Diamond Detailing — £297

The Diamond Detail is the comprehensive package — designed for drivers who want the best achievable result and longer-term protection from a single appointment.

Everything in the Gold Detail, plus:

  • Multi-stage paint inspection and preparation
  • Application of a premium long-duration paint protection coating
  • Full interior detail including leather conditioning where applicable
  • Engine bay clean available on request
  • Comprehensive glass treatment inside and out

The Diamond Detail is particularly well-suited for newer vehicles (establishing proper protection early), higher-value cars, or as a definitive reset after a period of neglect. The protection applied at this level is significantly more durable than standard wax — giving you a meaningfully longer interval before the next intensive session is needed.

Custom Project — Bespoke Pricing

Some situations don't fit neatly into a standard package. Fleet vehicles requiring multiple cars, show car preparation, specific paint correction work, or a combination of services — these are handled on a custom basis with clear, upfront pricing.

For anything outside the standard packages, the best approach is a conversation. Message us on WhatsApp with a description of your vehicle and what you're after — we'll give you an honest assessment and a clear quote without any upsell pressure.

Not Sure Which to Choose?

If you're still undecided, the simplest approach is to send us a message and describe your car's current condition. We'll advise on the right package — honestly, without pushing you towards a more expensive option if your car doesn't need it.

When you're ready to book: choose a slot via Cal.com for any of the three main packages. We cover Bristol, Bath, Clifton, Southville, Keynsham, Kingswood, Bedminster, and the surrounding area — and we come to you.
