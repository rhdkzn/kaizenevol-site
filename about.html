We only win when you win

We don’t ask you to pay on faith. Here’s exactly what we commit to when a brand comes on board.

01

One fee, agreed before we start

One monthly fee covers all of it: content, ads, social and email. You hear the price on the first call, not the third, and it is in writing before a pound is spent. Your ad money goes straight to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok from your own account. It never passes through us.

02

Judged on your profit

Before we spend anything we work out what one sale actually leaves you, after the item, the packaging, the postage, the fees and the returns, and how often a customer comes back. Every decision after that is judged on that number, not on what Instagram reports.

03

Direct access, fast response

You have direct access to us, not an account manager, not a junior. We reply within the same working day. Every time.

04

Five at a time, and you own everything

Five clients at a time, so every one of them gets the founders. And you own what’s yours: your ad account, your content, your customer list, and the tracking we set up on your shop. If we part ways, all of it stays with you.
