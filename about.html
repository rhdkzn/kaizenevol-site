We Only Win When You Win

This isn’t a standard agency retainer where you pay regardless of results. Here’s exactly what we commit to when you come on board.

  1. Result-focused from day one

    A 60-day performance guarantee: hit your agreed monthly target of booked clients, or your management fee is waived. We carry the risk from the first day your ads go live.

  2. Live in 7 days, no delays

    From discovery call to live campaign in 7 days. We don’t sit on work. If there’s a delay, it’ll be on our radar before you notice it.

  3. Direct access, fast response

    You have direct access to us — not an account manager, not a junior. We reply within the same working day. Every time.

  4. Your territory is locked

    Once you sign, no other business in your trade and local area gets access to this system. A kitchen specialist and a bathroom specialist in the same postcode aren’t competitors — but your direct competition is locked out for the duration of your engagement.
