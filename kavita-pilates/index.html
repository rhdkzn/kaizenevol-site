FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before your first class.

Not at all. Our Beginner Flow class is designed specifically for those new to Reformer Pilates. We recommend starting there — even if you've done mat pilates before, the Reformer is a different experience and the fundamentals matter.
Comfortable, fitted activewear you can move freely in. Grip socks are required — you can bring your own or purchase a pair at the studio. Avoid overly loose clothing as it can get caught on the machine.
All bookings are made online. Simply choose your class, pick your time, and secure your spot. Classes fill quickly, so we recommend booking a few days in advance. First-timers can also use the contact form below to get started.
We ask for at least 12 hours’ notice for cancellations. Late cancellations or no-shows within this window will be charged the full class fee. This helps keep the studio running and allows others on the waitlist to take the spot.
Reformer Pilates is widely used in rehabilitation. However, if you have an existing injury or medical condition, please let us know when booking. We'd typically recommend a private 1:1 session first so we can properly assess and programme for you.
The Reformer machine provides spring-based resistance, allowing for a much wider range of exercises than mat pilates. You'll work muscles you didn't know existed. Unlike a gym, there's no heavy lifting — just intelligent, controlled movement that builds real functional strength.
