Questions, Answered

How can it be this cheap when agencies charge thousands?
Agencies charge for weeks of meetings, revisions and project management. We've built our own tooling that lets us design and build a conversion-grade site in days, so the price reflects days of expert work — not months of overhead. You get a better site, faster, at a price that isn't padded.
What's included after launch?
Your first 30 days include free tweaks — words, prices, photos, opening hours — live within 48 hours. Your first 12 months of hosting and SSL are on us. After that, Forge Care (£39/mo, optional — founding 10 keep £29/mo for life) covers hosting, content edits within 48h and a monthly health check — or take the site anywhere you like, because you own it.
Who owns the website and the domain?
You do. Both. You pay once and the site is yours outright — the design, the words, the code, everything. Your domain name stays in your control, always. No rental model, no lock-in, no buyout fee, no small print.
What if I don't like what you build?
Then you don't pay — that's the whole point of building it first. You see the finished site before a penny changes hands. If it's close but not quite right, we'll revise it. If it's just not for you, you walk away owing nothing and we part as friends.
Do you write the words, or do I?
We do. Writing pages that turn visitors into enquiries is the core of what we do for our advertising clients. We'll take what you tell us in the 15-minute chat — your trade, your patch, what makes you the right choice — and turn it into copy that sounds like you and sells like us.
I already have a website. Can you replace it?
Yes — that's most of our work. We build the new site, you approve it, and we switch your existing domain over with no downtime and nothing technical on your plate. Your old site stays live until the moment the new one takes over.
Will it show up on Google?
Yes — and here's the shift most businesses have missed. Google now answers roughly 6 in 10 searches right on the results page, so being found no longer wins the job on its own. Every KaizenForge site is built on the fast, clean, mobile-first code Google rewards, with proper titles and descriptions — and the monthly health check keeps it that way. But its real job is to convert the visitors who do reach you into booked work, because those are the ones that count now. Want to actively pull in more traffic on top? That's exactly what our KaizenReach ad service is for.
